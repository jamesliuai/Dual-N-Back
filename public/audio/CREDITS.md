Changes: silence trimmed; mild noise reduction and 80 Hz high-pass/14 kHz low-pass filtering; consistent level normalization; short fades; encoded to mono 44.1 kHz 96 kbps MP3. Adapted September 12, 2026. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.
