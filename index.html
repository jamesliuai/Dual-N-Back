Free dual n-back memory training

Play dual n-back online, free and without an account. Follow a square and a spoken letter, and respond when either one repeats from a set number of rounds earlier.

What is dual n-back?

A working-memory task with two independent sequences: positions and sounds. The “n” is how many steps back you compare. In 1-back, compare with the previous round; in 2-back, with two rounds earlier.

How to play

Press A for a position match and L for an audio match, or use the on-screen buttons. Press both when both match, and nothing when neither does. A sound match does not imply a position match.

Your score

Catching matches and correctly ignoring non-matches count equally. The overall score averages position and audio. Warmup rounds are not scored, so compare sessions at the same level and pace.

Options

Choose 1-back to 9-back, a session length, and a pace from 1 to 5 seconds. Settings and your last 30 results are saved in this browser only.
